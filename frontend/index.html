How it works

This is a video processing application that uses YOLOv8 to detect and track the motion of a juggling ball alongside player body pose.

The detector is not fine-tuned for this specific use. It relies on the pre-trained COCO "sports ball" label (ID 32) for ball detection, and YOLOv8-Pose to detect the player's body joints.

To detect and count juggles, the algorithm uses a lightweight trajectory heuristic:

  • Detect the ball and player joints in each frame with spatial tracking.
  • Track whether the ball is Falling or Rising.
  • On trajectory reversal (from Falling to Rising), increment the count if the ball is within proximity of the player's body joints (feet, knees, head).

This algorithm is quite basic and has several limitations, such as the ball going out of the video frame or the player's fast movements that may be missed by the detection.

GPU acceleration (CUDA) is supported to speed up inference on compatible NVIDIA hardware.
